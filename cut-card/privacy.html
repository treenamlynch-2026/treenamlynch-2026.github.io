The Cut Card — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2026

Summary

The Cut Card does not collect, store off-device, or share any personal data. There is no account creation, no sign-in, and no data transmitted from your device to us or to any third party.

Data Collection

The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs of any kind. Gameplay settings (such as card size preference) are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted anywhere.

Third Parties

The Cut Card does not share data with third parties because it does not collect any data to share.

Children's Privacy

The Cut Card is rated 18+ due to simulated gambling content and is not directed at, or intended for use by, children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future update adds optional analytics or online features — this page will be updated and the in-app version notes will note the change.
