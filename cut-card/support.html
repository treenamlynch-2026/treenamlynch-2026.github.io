The Cut Card — Support

The Cut Card is a single-player blackjack practice app. Virtual chips only — no real-money wagering, no in-app purchases tied to gambling, no connection to any casino.

Common Questions

Do I need an account?
No. The app opens directly to the table — no sign-in required.

Is any real money involved?
No. All chips are virtual and have no cash value.

How do I change the card size?
Card size (Small, Standard, Large, Extra Large) is available from the in-app Settings.

What does the Shoe Tracker do?
It tracks the composition of the remaining shoe in real time, alongside the always-on basic-strategy display, so you can practice card counting and optimal play.
