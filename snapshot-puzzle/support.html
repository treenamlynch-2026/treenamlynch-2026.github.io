Common questions

How do I make a puzzle?
  1. Open Snapshot Puzzle and pick how many pieces you want.
  2. Tap Choose Photo and select a picture from your library.
  3. Drag the pieces into place to finish the picture.
My photos don't show up

Snapshot Puzzle needs permission to see your photos. Open the Settings app, tap Snapshot Puzzle, tap Photos, and choose Full Access or select the photos you want to use.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No. There's no sign-up, and the app works fully offline.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. Snapshot Puzzle runs on both iPhone and iPad.

The app crashed or froze

Close the app completely and reopen it. If it keeps happening, update to the latest version from the App Store, then email support with your device model and iOS version.
