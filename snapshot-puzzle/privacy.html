Privacy policy

Snapshot Puzzle. Effective September 27, 2026.

Snapshot Puzzle does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Your photos never leave your device.

Your photos

Snapshot Puzzle asks for access to your photo library so you can choose pictures to turn into puzzles. Photos are processed only on your device. They are not uploaded, copied to a server, or shared with anyone. You can change or remove photo access at any time in the Settings app under Snapshot Puzzle.

Data collection

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no ads. It does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising identifiers, and it works fully offline.

Third parties

No data is sent to or shared with third parties. Apple may provide anonymous crash reports and usage statistics to developers if you have opted in through your device settings; that data is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Because Snapshot Puzzle collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
