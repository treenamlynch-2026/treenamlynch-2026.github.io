Common questions

How do I color a page?
  1. Open a coloring book and choose a page.
  2. Pick a color from the palette.
  3. Tap an area to fill it, or switch to the brush to color freehand.
Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up, email or password. The app sets itself up automatically the first time you open it.

Are the coloring books free?

Yes. All coloring books in the current version are free.

New books aren't showing up

Books are downloaded over the internet. Check that you're connected, then close and reopen the app.

The app crashed or froze

Close the app completely and reopen it. If it keeps happening, update to the latest version from the App Store, then email support with your device model and iOS version.
